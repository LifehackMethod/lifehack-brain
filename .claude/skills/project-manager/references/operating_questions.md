# Operating Questions

Use these questions to design and maintain the project doc. Do not mechanically answer every question as a heading. Use them to infer the right structure.

## Goal and stakes

- What is the user trying to accomplish?
- Why does it matter?
- What outcome would count as success?
- What is the user's current priority?
- What constraints, deadlines, risks, or boundaries matter?

## Past

- What has already happened?
- What is known as fact?
- How do we know it?
- What source material, artifacts, or evidence exist?
- What actions have been taken?
- What tests or experiments have been run?
- What were the results?
- What did we learn?
- What decisions have been made?
- What assumptions were corrected?
- What paths should not be repeated?

## Present

- What is the current state?
- What is active right now?
- What is the current best understanding?
- What is the current working model?
- What hypotheses are live?
- What is being tested, drafted, decided, or built now?
- What is the user asking for in the current moment?
- What should be held stable while testing or deciding?

## Future

- What should happen next?
- What future tests, revisions, or actions are planned?
- What open questions remain?
- What would change the plan?
- What are the decision gates?
- What risks should be watched?
- What expert/stakeholder questions need to be asked?
- What artifacts should be produced or updated?

## Confidence and uncertainty

- Which claims are confirmed?
- Which claims are inferred?
- Which are hypotheses?
- Which are user hypotheses?
- Which are unknown?
- Which are deprecated?
- Are there conflicts in the record?
- What evidence would resolve each important uncertainty?

## Handoff

- What would a future AI need to know to continue without the original chat?
- What must not be lost?
- What context seems obvious now but will not be obvious later?
- What is the shortest accurate rehydration of the current state?
