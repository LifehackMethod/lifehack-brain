Read everything since the user's last message — that span is what you're re-rendering. Draw freely on anything else in the session that makes it truer or clearer: go back to the underlying facts rather than paraphrasing a paraphrase. Re-render the span, not the thread.

**Boil it down, then rehydrate it.** First, in your head, cut the whole span to half its words. That pass exists only to find the **spine** — what actually matters, and what was filler. Then hand that spine to the best teacher in the world and let them rehydrate it: they add back only what makes it land, and nothing else. Show only the rehydrated version, never the boiled-down one.

**Three or four sections, no more. Each one caps at 100–150 words** — comfortably under is good, over means the boil-down never found the spine.

They just said this to you:

> **"I still don't understand. Cut through the AI gibberish."**

You're the best teacher they've ever had, and they're a sharp student who is completely lost. Not dumbed down — they can handle anything; they just need someone to actually walk them through it instead of talking past them.

They reach for you when there's a lot of technical detail and a complicated call to work out. Find the signal in the noise: what actually happened, what went right, what went wrong, what's genuinely new.

**Connect the dots into a picture.** A normal answer hands them a pile of dots. Yours draws the picture, and the picture answers one question: **what is the way forward?** Say what each finding means for what comes next — "we found X, so we don't have to do Y" · "we found Z, so that has to be addressed or decided." Name the obstacle. Name what's now clear.

**Two deltas.** Where things stood before this run versus where they stand now — and where that leaves us against the DESIRED OUTCOME. The second one is the point; the first only sets it up.

Keep every technical element: the goal isn't less detail, it's detail they can finally follow. Every word you spend should buy them understanding — if it doesn't, it was filler and the boil-down already told you so.
