## Do this
1. **Sweep the ENTIRE scratchpad** — every open item, not just the ranked list. For each, ask: is this durable (it
   outlives this conversation), or was it just working chatter? Durable → it gets a destination below.
2. **Sort each durable item into ONE of four buckets:**
   - **CALENDAR** → Agent Ops events (today's *and* future-dated — flights, blocks, reminders). Never `primary`.
   - **TASKS** → new durable to-dos → their topical Google Tasks list.
   - **DURABLE RECORDS** → findings / decisions / system-debt that are NOT a calendar or task item — route to
     `<notes>/desks/cal/records/`, `<notes>/desks/cal/state/`, or root `<notes>/state/tech-debt.md`. **These have no
     calendar/task home and are exactly what vanished before — they MUST be filed, not dropped.**
     **DEFERRED TODAY is always a DURABLE RECORDS row** — the scratchpad's `## DEFERRED TODAY` block (lanes held from today's dominoes) routes here, destination: the diary stamp in Pass 5 (`<notes>/desks/cal/diary/YYYY/MM/DD.md`). It does NOT go to Tasks or the Life Map — diary-only.
   - **DAILY WIN** → the confirmed dominoes → **subtasks under the `✅ Daily Win:` task** in the Life Map (parent
     `$(python3 "$ROOT/shared/cal_config.py" --get daily_parent_task)`), so the day's plan lands under the Win.
3. **DEDUP — don't double-write.** Mark any item already written earlier this session (or already a live task/event) as
   **DONE — skip**, not a new row. When unsure, keep it (the clerk's read-back is the final check) — preserve, never
   guess-drop.
4. **Build the expanded WRITE-LEDGER** — one row per intended write/file, each tagged with its bucket + dedup status,
   every row starting ❌. This (not the dominoes alone) is what the clerk drains in Pass 5.
5. **Present the ledger for their OK** — **"Here's everything I'm about to commit + file — confirm, or cut any line."**
   Only his word advances. (This is the human-in-the-loop gate; the clerk that follows is mechanical.)

STOP-CHECK: the WHOLE scratchpad was swept (not just dominoes); every durable item has a bucket; dedup applied; the
expanded WRITE-LEDGER is built and **confirmed by the person**. The scratchpad is **NOT deleted here** — that happens in
