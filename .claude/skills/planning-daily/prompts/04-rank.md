# PASS 4 — RANK (the dominoes, then Olsen)

TRIPWIRE: don't write anything here. Ranking produces a PLAN; writing is Pass 5, only after the person confirms.
A wrong plan executed beautifully is still wrong.

This is the one place the interrogative process is allowed to surface SUGGESTIONS — and they're still their to confirm.

## Do this
1. **Fuse** the whole scratchpad world model — including Pass 3's physical-reality constraints.
2. **One move per LIVE lane** — the single highest-leverage move (best guess). Quiet/handled lanes get nothing.
   Apply free/busy + declined (don't rank a dead or already-done item) AND logistics (don't rank a move the body
   can't fit — Pass 3 flagged it).
3. **Weigh against the Life Map** (READ-ONLY) — lean leverage toward where they are going. Never edit it.
4. **Rank into ONE balanced, doable list — the dominoes.** Cover the live bases, don't let one lane dominate. Each:
   a **1–3 word ALL-CAPS tag** + the move + one line of why. Order by leverage (even doing only the top one is the highest-value base).
5. **Propose the Daily Win** — they SET it. (In Pass 5 the clerk writes the confirmed dominoes as **subtasks under
   the `✅ Daily Win:` task** in the Life Map, via the guarded Daily-Win exception — so the day's plan lands under the
   Win. Here you only propose + confirm; no writes.)
5a. **THE SKEPTIC BEAT — one adversarial pass before the gate.**
   This is NOT a re-ranking. It surfaces tension; it does NOT propose a replacement Win. Two sharp lines, then one question:
   - **The case this is the WRONG Win:** steelman the strongest argument that the proposed Daily Win is the wrong focus for today — a competing lane with higher urgency, a dependency that makes today's Win moot, a risk that today's pick ignores.
   - **One thing that could blow the dominoes miss:** name the single assumption baked into the ranked list that, if false, collapses the whole plan. (Travel time? a call that cancels? a dependency not yet confirmed?)
   - **Ask exactly ONE question** — the sharpest one that would change the plan if answered differently.
   **THE LAW: name no alternative Win. Surface the tension; let them hold it.**
6. **Present as a PLAN TO CONFIRM** + your suggestion: **"Confirm this as your day, or change it? — I suggest [X]."**
   On confirm, **capture the agreed dominoes + the Daily Win verbatim into the scratchpad** so the consolidate beat can commit them.

## Then — NAME-WHAT-YOU'RE-SKIPPING
After the dominoes are confirmed, surface the DEFERRED TODAY list: every live-board lane that has a real item but is NOT in today's dominoes. Format each as:

```
## DEFERRED TODAY
LANE/item → held because [one-line reason]
```

One row per held item. No editorializing; no re-ranking; no second-guessing the choice. This is a ledger, not a debate. **Capture this list verbatim into the scratchpad** — it must survive into the DURABLE RECORDS bucket in Pass 4.5 and land in the diary stamp (Pass 5). It does NOT touch the Life Map or Google Tasks — diary-only.

## Then — the OLSEN closing beat
After the dominoes (once confirmed/adjusted) and the DEFERRED TODAY list is rendered, close in **Olsen's voice** (read `<notes>/desks/cal/skill-refs/olsen.md`). A SEPARATE
grounding voice — NOT Cal's. He does NOT touch the *what*; he grounds the *why*: one-day-at-a-time, the Serenity-
Prayer line (what the person controls vs. can't), the read on "the one thing" (protect the standing non-negotiable
commitment first; the rest is gravy). Salty + warm, says just enough. Tie it to today's actual dominoes.

STOP-CHECK: the person confirmed the ranked list + the Daily Win (edits folded + re-confirmed); both captured verbatim into
the scratchpad; Olsen close rendered. **No writes happen here — the WHOLE scratchpad (not just the dominoes) gets
consolidated into the WRITE-LEDGER at the next beat.**
