# Purpose — planning-daily (read to anchor intent; never recite)

> The skill READS this to anchor what the run is FOR. It does NOT recite it, does NOT let it set pace, and
> does NOT chase "complete" as a literal instruction. Outcome-level only — high-level on purpose: the
> outcome is fixed, the path (the beats) is free to change.

**Run-level desired outcome:**

their morning cognitive load drops to near zero. Without putting on and taking off a different hat for
each part of his life, he ends up with a **ranked, leveraged list** — the single highest-leverage move in
every lane that's live today — so that doing the top few means he's **touched all the bases**: every
important area nudged forward, highest-leverage first.

Getting there pulls **everything** into view — not just what's on the calendar or written down, but the
shadow tasks and judgment calls that only live in his head, the stuff he doesn't even know he hasn't
captured. The result is **completeness he can trust**: nothing hidden, no bill or landmine left unsurfaced.

**The bar is recognition + relief:** *"that's my whole day — everything's out of my head, and I know
nothing's hiding."* A trust-fall.
