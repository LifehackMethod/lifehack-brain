# Phase 5 · Step 1 — Calendarize (Brilliant-EA fill)  ·  HUD `[5/6] ACTION · Calendarize`

**Paint the HUD on entry:** `bash "$ROOT/system/tools/skill_hud.sh" set '🧭 Cal · Weekly   [5/6] ACTION · Calendarize · body-first fill · next → Report'`

**Objective:** draft the ENTIRE week as a best-guess — never a blank page — then correct it round by round to the
masterpiece bar. **Fill order is non-negotiable: body first.**

## The fill
1. **Body first:** buffer, meals, recovery onto the calendar BEFORE work.
2. **The Win** into the best-energy window (whenever he operates at peak).
3. **Shallow work** batched into low-energy blocks (sized as bundles, not N items).
Tools: locked blocks vs puzzle-piece (movable) blocks · energy zones · clustering related work · flex time.

**Two purposes (a borrowed framework):** *Floor* — marry time-supply to time-demand; commitment debt (promised-but-
unscheduled) kills weeks. *Ceiling* — the best-return arrangement across time · energy · happiness · lifestyle
design · productivity. Not just fitting the work in, fitting it in WELL.

**Fresh free/busy pull:** this step needs a live free/busy read from the calendar — the ONE point the session
reaches live state (you can't lay out a week against a stale store).

**Correction rounds:** present a day-by-day sketch + numbered corrections; he corrects; repeat until it's a
calendar he'd stand up and clap for. Every placement goes to the **WRITE-LEDGER** on the scratchpad (a queue of
confirmed actions) — NOTHING is written to the live calendar here.

## do NOT
- do NOT write to the live calendar — queue to the WRITE-LEDGER only (the clerk writes in step 3).
- do NOT put work before body — body/buffer/recovery first, always.
- do NOT present a blank page — commit a full best-guess and let him correct.
- do NOT drop into per-item triage (scope guard) — defer to Phase 6.

## Output contract
The WRITE-LEDGER on the scratchpad holds every confirmed placement (calendar rows, tagged Agent-Ops).
