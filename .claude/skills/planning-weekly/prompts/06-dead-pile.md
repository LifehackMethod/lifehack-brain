**Objective:** move anything whose OWN evidence says done-or-moot straight to the bulk pile with ZERO extra
attention — no re-reading, no deciding. These are items whose fate is obvious: a past due-date with notes confirming
it shipped, an event that already happened (check the item's own dates, not just calendar presence — old events fall
outside the window), known noise/vendor-admin senders.

Dead items get NO individual treatment — they queue for the one bulk gesture in the next step.

## do NOT
- do NOT spend attention deciding a dead item — if it needs a judgment call, it's NOT dead; leave it for bulk/individual.
- do NOT execute here — dead items are archived (destructive) only via the approved bulk gesture next step.
- do NOT dead-pile a human/tender thread or anything with a live ask (those route to individually-handle).

## Output contract
The dead pile is identified + queued for bulk; count noted on the scratchpad. `✅ dead-pile cleared to bulk`.
