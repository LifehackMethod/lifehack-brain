brief**: the full ranked plan + world model, framed as *"our best current thinking, not prescriptive — challenge
it."* That framing is the de-bias. **No adversarial / "red-team" language** — a neutral charge (the label makes a
member perform adversarialism instead of thinking).

**Every member returns, and nothing else:**
```

   · disposition-hint: fold-in | loop-back-to-P3 | hold

```
