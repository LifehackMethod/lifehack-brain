# Phase 3 — Prioritization  ·  HUD `[3/6] PRIORITIZATION`

**Paint the HUD on entry:** `bash "$ROOT/system/tools/skill_hud.sh" set '🧭 Cal · Weekly   [3/6] PRIORITIZATION · painting the portrait · next → The Council'`

**Desired outcome:** the machine PAINTS THE PORTRAIT — turns all the connected dots into a picture and asks "do
you recognize this as your real life?" The plan zippers **top-down** (year → quarter → month → this week), never
bottom-up. The Win is whatever makes everything else easier or unnecessary; the task list is commitment debt, not
the goal. the person is minimally in the loop — reports mode, mostly recognition — but he is the decider on the Win
ranking and the bulk-action approvals.

**Rolling scratchpad view first:** show Phase 2's additions (the confirmed world model + Human Delta).

**Standing rule — micro-report + signal-through-noise:** before every HITL moment, run a micro-report — (a) what
he said he wanted, (b) where he is on the arc (year→quarter→month→week). Every report shows patterns/themes/risks,
NEVER a raw dump ("Monday is X, Tuesday is Y" is banned).

## The three reports — deliver as THREE separate readouts (not one long combined one; long readouts get shortened)

**Report 1 — Radical Clarity (goals + the Win).** Arc first ("this year X → quarter Y → month D → so HERE this
week"). Then the Win, generated by the **9 leverage-angle fan-out**: dispatch nine fresh-context sonnet sub-agents
(`leverage-angles/*.md`), each nominating a candidate Win by a different currency (panel-independence: distinct
role, "a claim to defeat," under-specified method, aggregated blind). Dedupe + rank the candidates → present the
ranked list → **the person reorders/corrects; that correction IS the reconciliation** (not a separate step). Don't
over-trust unanimous agreement (same-model false consensus). If a filled lanes file exists → guess the #1 move
per lane and rank across lanes; else fall back to one main Win + ≤2 Bonus Aims. Keep the Win test LOOSE.

**Report 2 — Leverage × Urgency 2×2 (task triage, not processing).** Tasks as themes/initiatives/outcomes, never
a raw N-item list. Zones: Hi-lev/Lo-urg → **Win Zone (protect)** · Hi-lev/Hi-urg → **Must Deliver** ·
Lo-lev/Hi-urg → **Shallow Trap / OPP (bundle+batch)** · Lo-lev/Lo-urg → **Kill or Push (bulk-defer)**. Bulk actions
(reschedule lo/lo, mark implied-complete, add Phase-2 shadow tasks) need his approval **at the group/theme level**,
never per item. Then the capacity stress-test: task-load vs the time supply from Phase 1 — the plan won't lock
until it fits; shallow work bundles into 30–60-min blocks. This is 5,000-ft triage, explicitly NOT processing to
zero (that's optional Phase 6).

**Report 3 — Calendar Clarity.** Signal-through-noise on the calendar: pinch points, over-scheduling, slack,
collisions, shadow calendar items ("Fridays are with my kids"). Compute time supply (free hours) paired with
Report 2's task-load → the deficit/surplus read. Decide WHAT should land on the calendar — the **write happens in
Phase 5**; Phase 3 produces the plan-of-intent only.

**Steal 3 (checkable-Win bar) — pending outside review:** build Report 1 without it; fold in if it's approved.

## do NOT

- do NOT write anything live — calendar/tasks/Life-Map writes are Phase 5. Notate the plan-of-intent to the pad.
- do NOT dump raw lists — themes and signal only.
- do NOT anticipate Phase 4/5 — end at the ranked, biased-by-design plan.
