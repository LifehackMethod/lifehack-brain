# Phase 6 · Step 1 — Fires First  ·  HUD `[6/6] TRIAGE · Fires`

**Paint the HUD on entry:** `bash "$ROOT/system/tools/skill_hud.sh" set '🧭 Cal · Weekly   [6/6] TRIAGE · Fires · next → dead-pile'`

**Objective:** surface the FIRES before anything else — items that meet ANY of: a real consequence ≤48h · a named
person blocked waiting on the person · a live commitment visibly slipping. These get handled first, regardless of anything.

Each fire = an individual **card** (never bulked): Source `[email|task|calendar|both]` · What · Why hot · **Cal's
best-guess action** (a real proposed move; if the fix is deep work, propose the calendar block for it) · Your call.
Pull the item's real detail before proposing (never off a bare title). Anchor "hot" to real priority, not generic
urgency — a `modified_at` bump is not activity; a reply/comment from someone who isn't the person is.

## do NOT
- do NOT bulk a fire; do NOT execute — propose the card, the person calls it.
- do NOT decide off a title; do NOT treat a timestamp bump as a live signal.

## Output contract
Fire cards presented + the person's calls swept to the scratchpad. `✅ fires handled`.
