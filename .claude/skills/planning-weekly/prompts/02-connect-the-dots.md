# Phase 2 — Connect the Dots / World Model Inventory  ·  HUD `[2/6] CONNECT THE DOTS`

**Paint the HUD on entry:** `bash "$ROOT/system/tools/skill_hud.sh" set '🧭 Cal · Weekly   [2/6] CONNECT THE DOTS · mining the gaps · next → Prioritization'`

**Desired outcome:** by the end, the scratchpad is a FULL image of the person's on-the-ground reality — the dots
connected, the shadow information surfaced. The key HITL moment is him confirming that picture: "here's what I
see" → "yes, you got it."

**Rolling scratchpad view first:** show what Phase 1 wrote (the reality-reset · confirmed/temporary monthly goal ·
marked deltas). Confirm the pad is live, then begin.

## Run — two jobs at once
- **Connect:** take the known dots (tasks, emails, events from the Map) and find the threads between them. Five venue tasks may = "you booked a job." A "work block" may actually be a family event. Commit the read; ask him to correct it.
- **Mine the shadow:** surface what only lives in his head — shadow *information* (a conversation, an intention, context that changes what a task means), not just shadow tasks.

**The leveraged-question rule (the craft — this is the whole phase):** every question is
1) **deeply informed** (only askable by something that did the homework),
2) **well-considered** (moves understanding the most right now), and
3) **easy to answer** (a fact/memory/reaction he already holds — never makes him do your synthesis).
Ask the **minimum number of questions that fill the maximum number of gaps.** Questions are **numbered**, each
carries your **best-guess answer**, and he answers by number. The game continues until gaps are filled — a deep
question that gets a shallow answer earns a follow-up ("that's not enough — I need more"). **Exit backstop (bounded —
never an infinite loop):** cap at the adaptive round count (below), AND the person's "move on" ALWAYS advances the phase —
carry any still-open item forward to the scratchpad as a `⚠ gap: <question>` note (never erase it).

**SMART filter** on anything surfaced as a task: Specific · Measurable · Actionable · Realistic · Time-bound —
so milestones ("finish the Springfield project") don't get confused with this-week tasks ("call Jordan for the mail").

**Adaptive rounds:** Phase 0's size/confidence readout sets the count — lightly-annotated week = 2–3 questions;
two months un-reviewed = a heavy pass. Not prescriptive.

**Lens order:** rank-biggest-first across all lenses (biggest Conflicts → biggest Lane gaps → biggest
Audit/biological findings), NOT separate structured rounds per lens.

**Flywheel write-back:** every round, sweep his delta into the scratchpad AND (when the HITL-note store exists,
Phase D) write it back as a HITL note onto the source item, so next week those items aren't re-asked.

**Reach the corpus only when unsure:** need a full email body / task detail → spin the on-demand full-read reader
(`_full-read-reader.md`, sonnet) to fetch just the facts; the raw body never enters this window.

## do NOT
- do NOT name the Win, rank, or conclude — this phase COLLECTS dots and surfaces tensions ONLY (the Win is Phase 3).
- do NOT cram multiple questions into one run-on paragraph — numbered, best-guess-attached, answerable-by-number.
- do NOT make him author your synthesis ("what's the lesson?" is banned — you name it, he confirms).
- do NOT accept a shallow answer to a load-bearing question — follow up.

## Output contract
Scratchpad = a full connected picture of the week's reality + the confirmed Human Delta. `✅ phase 2 complete`.
