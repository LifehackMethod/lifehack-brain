# Phase 6 · Step 5 — Park + Wrap-Tally  ·  HUD `[6/6] TRIAGE · Park/Wrap`

**Paint the HUD on entry:** `bash "$ROOT/system/tools/skill_hud.sh" set '🧭 Cal · Weekly   [6/6] TRIAGE · wrap · next → done'`

**Objective:** parking is a **first-class exit**, available at ANY point in the sweep — not just here. If the person is
done, offer it plainly: "Inbox at N left — one-gesture sheet now, or park them?" Parking is a clean stop, **never a
failure, never guilt, never a forced march to zero.** Record `parked: <n> items <date>` on the scratchpad; the next
run picks up from the park point.

**Wrap-tally (the close):** one honest line — **"N resolved · M drafted (awaiting your send) · K kept · P parked."**
Then: confirm every approved action actually executed (read-back), and clear Phase 6's HUD.

## do NOT
- do NOT guilt the person toward zero or force a march; parking is a legitimate, clean exit.
- do NOT report an action done without reading it back; do NOT leave an approved-but-unexecuted action dangling.

## Output contract
Wrap-tally rendered; parked items recorded; approved actions verified executed. Phase 6 complete —
`bash "$ROOT/system/tools/skill_hud.sh" clear`. `✅ phase 6 complete`.

**NEXT:** the weekly + triage run is done. (If Cal's anchor/scratch_flag are still armed from the weekly, they were
cleared at Phase 5's Act step; if Phase 6 ran in a fresh session, clear any armed there.)
