# Triage guide — Leverage×Urgency (Phase 3, Report 2 mechanic)

> Cal reads this before Phase 3's Report 2 and uses it to place candidate moves. Do NOT use Eisenhower here
> — that's the monthly mechanic. The axes and zone names below are the ONLY valid vocabulary, and they match
> the `03-prioritization.md` driver. Triage is by THEME/initiative, never a raw N-item list.

---

## The two axes

**Leverage** — how much does this item compound toward the **Monthly Win**?
- **High leverage:** directly advances the Monthly Win, or removes a blocker slowing it. A win here creates
  momentum that carries forward.
- **Low leverage:** necessary but doesn't move the month's needle (maintenance, admin, low-value noise).

**Urgency** — how time-bound is this item?
- **High urgency:** a real external deadline this week, or becomes significantly harder/impossible if not
  done this week (a meeting date, a client window, a perishable opportunity).
- **Low urgency:** no hard deadline this week; can be scheduled or pushed without real consequence.

Both axes are **binary for the sort** (High / Low). If something is genuinely medium, name the uncertainty
and ask the person — don't force it.

---

## The 4 zones (canonical names — match the driver)

| | **High Urgency** | **Low Urgency** |
|---|---|---|
| **High Leverage** | **MUST DELIVER** (fight for calendar time) | **WIN ZONE** (protect) |
| **Low Leverage** | **SHALLOW TRAP / OPP** (bundle + batch) | **KILL OR PUSH** (bulk-defer) |

### MUST DELIVER — High-Lev / High-Urg
Non-negotiable this week. The Win and Bonus Aims come from the high-leverage side; a Must-Deliver item that
IS the week's highest leverage becomes the Win, the rest (up to 2) ride as Bonus Aims. If this zone is
overloaded, something is mis-classified — surface it and interrogate.

### WIN ZONE — High-Lev / Low-Urg (PROTECT)
The compounding work that isn't urgent yet — the zone most likely to get sacrificed to the urgent. The whole
point of naming it is to **protect a time block for it**, not let it drift. Ask: "where in the week does this
land?" A Win-Zone item with no block is a future emergency.

### SHALLOW TRAP / OPP — Low-Lev / High-Urg
Feels urgent because of social pressure — Other People's Priorities. Real urgency, but the leverage isn't
yours. **Cap it** (one block) and **bundle/batch** similar items so they don't fragment the week.

### KILL OR PUSH — Low-Lev / Low-Urg (BULK-DEFER)
Consciously not this week. A practiced triager clears 40–60% of the list here in one counted gesture. These become the
NOT-THIS-WEEK list in the Radical Clarity Report (Phase 5). Not failure — naming what you're NOT doing is the
discipline. Cal holds these without editorializing; the person can reclaim any at any time (bulk-defer needs his
group-level approval before it commits).

## Worked examples

**Example 1 — Consulting pitch deck (MUST DELIVER)**
> Leverage: HIGH — the client presentation is the path to the Monthly Win (new retainer). Urgency: HIGH —
> meeting is Thursday. Zone: MUST DELIVER → Win candidate.

**Example 2 — ClaudeOps weekly skill build (WIN ZONE)**
> Leverage: HIGH — compounds operational capability (a lever on everything). Urgency: LOW — no external
> deadline this week. Zone: WIN ZONE → ask the person to assign a specific block (protect it).

**Example 3 — Dentist appointment admin (SHALLOW TRAP / OPP)**
> Leverage: LOW — maintenance. Urgency: HIGH — paperwork due Friday. Zone: SHALLOW TRAP → cap at 30 min;
> batch with other admin into one block.

## Common mis-classifications to catch
- **"Important" ≠ "High Leverage."** Important to the person personally ≠ compounding toward the Monthly Win. A
  recovery/personal item may be critically important but low-leverage toward the Work Win. Place it
  accurately; name the distinction; let the person decide. *(Capacity/rest can still be the Win — see the
  Capacity leverage angle + the Touchdown Dance council member.)*
- **"Feels urgent" ≠ "High Urgency."** No real external deadline → likely Low-Urg → WIN ZONE is the honest zone.
- **MUST DELIVER should not be overloaded.** If it is, the sort is wrong or the Monthly Win is too broad.
  Surface it; interrogate; don't accept a bloated zone.
