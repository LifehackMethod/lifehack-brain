# Triage guide — Leverage × Urgency (step 5's mechanic)

> The session reads this before sorting tasks into zones (step 5) and uses it to place candidate
> moves. Do NOT use the Eisenhower matrix here — that is the MONTHLY mechanic. The axes and zone
> names below are the only valid vocabulary, and they match `references/zones.md` (the one
> definition; this file is its source and its working detail). Triage is by THEME/initiative, never
> a raw N-item list.

## The two axes

**Leverage** — how much does this item compound toward the **monthly goal**?
- **High leverage:** directly advances the monthly goal, or removes a blocker slowing it. A win here
  creates momentum that carries forward.
- **Low leverage:** necessary but does not move the month's needle (maintenance, admin, low-value noise).

**Urgency** — how time-bound is this item?
- **High urgency:** a real external deadline this week, or becomes significantly harder or impossible
  if not done this week (a meeting date, a client window, a perishable opportunity).
- **Low urgency:** no hard deadline this week; can be scheduled or pushed without real consequence.

Both axes are **binary for the sort** (High / Low). If something is genuinely medium, name the
uncertainty, lower the confidence mark, and let the person rule — don't force it.

## The 4 zones (canonical names — match the driver; full definitions in references/zones.md)

| | **High Urgency** | **Low Urgency** |
|---|---|---|
| **High Leverage** | **MUST DELIVER** (fight for calendar time) | **WIN ZONE** (protect) |
| **Low Leverage** | **SHALLOW TRAP / OPP** (bundle + batch) | **KILL OR PUSH** (bulk-defer) |

### MUST DELIVER — High-Lev / High-Urg
Non-negotiable this week. The Win and Bonus Aims come from the high-leverage side; a Must-Deliver item
that IS the week's highest leverage becomes the Win, the rest (up to 2) ride as Bonus Aims. If this
zone is overloaded, something is mis-classified — surface it and interrogate.

### WIN ZONE — High-Lev / Low-Urg (PROTECT)
The compounding work that isn't urgent yet — the zone most likely to get sacrificed to the urgent. The
whole point of naming it is to **protect a time block for it**, not let it drift. Ask: "where in the
week does this land?" A Win-Zone item with no block is a future emergency.

### SHALLOW TRAP / OPP — Low-Lev / High-Urg
Feels urgent because of social pressure — Other People's Priorities. Real urgency, but the leverage
isn't yours. **Cap it** (one block) and **bundle/batch** similar items so they don't fragment the week.

### KILL OR PUSH — Low-Lev / Low-Urg (BULK-DEFER)
Consciously not this week. A practiced triager clears 40–60% of the list here in one counted gesture.
These become the NOT-THIS-WEEK list in the final read-back. Not failure — naming what you're NOT doing
is the discipline. The session holds these without editorializing; the person can reclaim any at any
time (bulk-defer needs the person's group-level approval before it commits).

## Worked examples

**Example 1 — A client proposal (MUST DELIVER)**
> Leverage: HIGH — the client presentation is the path to the monthly goal (a new retainer). Urgency:
> HIGH — meeting is Thursday. Zone: MUST DELIVER → Win candidate.

**Example 2 — A compounding capability build (WIN ZONE)**
> Leverage: HIGH — compounds operational capability (a lever on everything). Urgency: LOW — no
> external deadline this week. Zone: WIN ZONE → ask the person to assign a specific block (protect it).

**Example 3 — Appointment admin (SHALLOW TRAP / OPP)**
> Leverage: LOW — maintenance. Urgency: HIGH — paperwork due Friday. Zone: SHALLOW TRAP → cap at
> 30 min; batch with other admin into one block.

## Common mis-classifications to catch
- **"Important" ≠ "High Leverage."** Important to the person personally ≠ compounding toward the
  monthly goal. A recovery or personal item may be critically important but low-leverage toward the
  month's goal. Place it accurately; name the distinction; let the person decide. *(Capacity/rest can
  still be the Win — see the Capacity leverage angle and the Touchdown Dance council advisor.)*
- **"Feels urgent" ≠ "High Urgency."** No real external deadline → likely Low-Urg → WIN ZONE is the
  honest zone.
- **MUST DELIVER should not be overloaded.** If it is, the sort is wrong or the monthly goal is too
  broad. Surface it; interrogate; don't accept a bloated zone.
