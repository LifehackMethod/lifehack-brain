# Purpose — planning-weekly (read to anchor intent; never recite)

> The skill READS this to anchor what the run is FOR. It does NOT recite it, does NOT let it set pace,
> and does NOT chase "complete" as a literal instruction. Outcome-level only: the outcome is fixed, the
> path (the beats) is free to change. *(Layers 1+2 per the skill-building SOP §0.5; locked in the design
> bible `desks/cal/planning-weekly-redesign-record.md` Decision 15, enriched with the compounding-knowledge
> frame approved 2026-07-13.)*

## Layer 1 — the user's outcome + the bar

The person's weekly planning load drops to near zero — every lane leaves with its one highest-leverage move,
the week on his calendar, nothing left in his head. The machine does all it can and automates the rote,
but the data is only **dots**; the **picture** — the threads, the judgment, the shadow info — lives only
in his head. So human-in-the-loop is **deliberate, not a gap**: it *mines* him for that gold, made easy,
and consolidates everything into **one trusted place**, the week handled like an EA planned it.

This run is also **one beat of a bigger machine.** The cadence check-ins (daily→yearly) together build a
**compounding, time-indexed record of reality** — what actually happened, what's meant to happen next.
That record serves the person, but its more important consumer is **the LLM itself**: a trustable ground truth
it reasons from instead of guessing. So a weekly run isn't just "plan the week" — it's **turning the
machine's low-confidence guesses into human-confirmed truth** and banking it so the next cadence up (and
every future session) can stand on it.

**Bar:** *"it's all in one place I trust — I can turn my head off and glide the week"* — AND a cold LLM
session could load this week's record and treat it as ground truth.

## Layer 2 — the skill's role

Cal delivers it as a **detective** (one voice; Olsen only at the very end) — an **interrogative
exploration that stays away from solutions, conclusions, and overconfidence; it never jumps to an
answer.** It treats the full pull (the Grand Central dots, distilled into the night-before **Map**) as a
**guess** and **mines him for what only he holds.** Any conclusion waits for the very end — if ever — and only when the human
calls for it. It **writes the gray matter every turn; consolidates before writing.**

**Place on the autonomy spectrum: human-in-the-loop by design.** The machine does everything automatable
and reserves the human ONLY for what only he can supply — the picture, the judgment, the shadow info —
and makes giving it effortless. "Maximalist intake, minimalist commitment; the human keeps the pen."

**The grain is cadence-health**, not re-routing: "is the week structured to move the month's Win?" It does
NOT change course on the DCA (quarterly only) — if something seems to warrant a course change, flag it and
hold it for the monthly/quarterly run.
