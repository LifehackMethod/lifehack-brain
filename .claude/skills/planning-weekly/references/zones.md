# The four zones — the ONE definition

Every prompt, subagent file or script that names a zone points here. It is never redefined anywhere
else; if a file you are reading defines a zone differently, this file wins.

Leverage × Urgency is the WEEKLY version of the well-known "important × urgent" matrix, with
"important" replaced by **leverage**: *does it compound toward the monthly goal?* (The Eisenhower
matrix itself is the MONTHLY tool.) Both axes are binary for the sort — an item is either high or low
leverage, and either has or has not a real deadline or consequence this week. An item genuinely
between two zones gets a best guess with a lowered confidence mark and a named reason — never a
forced fit.

| Zone | Leverage | Urgency | What it means for the week |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Win-zone** | high | **LOW** | protect it — it goes in first, with a real block, before anything urgent can eat it. Ask of every Win-zone item: "where in the week does this land?" A Win-zone item with no block is a future emergency. |
| **Must-deliver** | high | high | scheduled and delivered this week — fight for its calendar time. If this zone is overloaded, something is mis-classified; surface it, never accept a bloated zone. |
| **Shallow-trap** | low | high | feels urgent because of other people's priorities. Cap it and bundle similar items into a few batched blocks, so it never fragments the week. |
| **Kill-or-push** | low | low | kill it, or push it out of the week — a conscious choice, listed in full as the NOT-THIS-WEEK list. Naming what you are NOT doing is the discipline, not a failure. |

Common mis-classifications to catch:
- **"Important" is not "high leverage."** Important to the person personally is not automatically
  compounding toward the monthly goal. Place it accurately; let the person move it. (Rest and
  recovery can still be the leverage — a protected block for the body is a legitimate Win-zone item.)
- **"Feels urgent" is not "high urgency."** No real deadline or consequence this week → low urgency →
  the honest zone for compounding work is Win-zone.
